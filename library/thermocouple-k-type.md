# K-type thermocouple (summary)

**A K-type thermocouple taped to the cell body is the way this kit watches
temperature during a charge or discharge test.** Stop a test that the
cell's charge or discharge temperature limit does not allow; see
`cell-18650.md`.

## Range and accuracy

| Value                | Limit                                     |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Measurement range    | -40 °C to 250 °C                          |
| Typical accuracy     | ± 1.5 °C, or ± 0.4 %, whichever is larger |
| Response time (bead) | About 0.5 s in still air                  |

## Placement

- Tape the bead to the cell's flat can, away from the load resistor and any
  other heat source, so the reading is the cell's temperature and not the
  load's.
- A cycling test plan should name the sample interval and the temperature
  at which to stop, in addition to the electrical acceptance criteria.
