# 3641AS: the display

Read datasheets/3641as.md for the standard pinout.

- **The standard 3641AS has 12 pins: four digit commons and eight segment
  pins.** Source: datasheets/3641as.md. Confidence: medium.
- **The kit drives its display with eight lines through seven 47 Ω resistors,
  so the kit wiring differs from the standard pinout, or the part differs.**
  Source: datasheets/fm-radio-kit-schematic.md. Confidence: low.
