# 8002: the audio amplifier

Read datasheets/hxj8002.md for the pins and the limits.

- **The supply is 2.2 to 5.5 V, and the absolute maximum is 6.0 V.** Source:
  datasheets/hxj8002.md, page 5. Confidence: medium.
- **The kit ties SHUTDOWN (pin 1) to GND, so the amplifier is always on.**
  Source: datasheets/fm-radio-kit-schematic.md. Confidence: medium.
- **The two outputs drive the speaker as a bridge. Neither output is a ground
  reference.** Source: datasheets/hxj8002.md, page 13. Confidence: medium.
- **The speaker is 4 Ω and 3 W. The power figures of the datasheet apply at 5
  V, and the kit supplies 3.7 to 4.8 V.** Source:
  datasheets/fm-radio-kit-product.md and datasheets/hxj8002.md. Confidence: medium.
