# The bands of the radio

- **The product page gives a search range of 50 to 108 MHz.** Source:
  datasheets/fm-radio-kit-product.md. Confidence: low.
- **The tuner chip defaults to 87 to 108 MHz. The band bits that the stock
  firmware sets decide the range, and nobody has read them.** Source:
  datasheets/rda5807fp.md. Confidence: medium.
