# The audio path

- **LOUT and ROUT each pass a 100 µF capacitor to the headphone jack PJ1.**
  Source: library/fm-radio-kit-schematic.md. Confidence: medium.
- **The switched contacts of the jack join at the net IN, which feeds the
  amplifier. A plug opens the contacts and silences the speaker.** Source:
  library/fm-radio-kit-schematic.md, and the manual, page 15. Confidence:
  medium.
