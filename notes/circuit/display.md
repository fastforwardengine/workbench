# The display circuit

- **Eight drive lines (D1 to D7 and S0) connect the microcontroller to the
  display, and seven of them pass through 47 Ω resistors.** Source:
  datasheets/fm-radio-kit-schematic.md. Confidence: medium.
- **A plain 4-digit common-cathode display needs twelve lines, so the wiring
  or the part differs from the standard one.** Source: datasheets/3641as.md.
  Confidence: medium.
